## 1. Scope

1. Includes all **facilities, workshops, rented premises, and garages** run by the association.
2. Collectively referred to as **"facilities"** or **"facility"**.
3. The signer of these terms is referred to as **"member"** or **"members"**.

## 2. Access and Opening Hours

1. Membership grants **access to facilities, machines, and tools** during opening hours.
2. Certain machines/tools require a **license** to operate independently or under supervision.
3. See paragraph **"15. LICENSE"** for further information.
4. **Opening hours** are dynamic and can change weekly as they are operated by members.

## 3. Up time

1. High usage can put pressure on **machines and tools**, causing **waiting times**.
2. Machines/tools may be **"out of service"** until repaired or replaced.
3. Members must inform **employees, volunteers, or representatives** of breakdowns to keep others informed.

## 4. Rights of members

1. Right to **vote** in the yearly election of the **Committee** (one vote each).
2. Right to **run for election** in the yearly Committee election.
3. Right to **participate in all open meetings** hosted by the Forening.
4. Right to **appeal a ban** by sending an application to the Board (must wait at least 60 days between applications).

## 5. Damages

1. Members must inform **representatives** if they damage a **machine**, so it can be dealt with quickly.
2. **Improper or irresponsible use** of machines/tools/facilities will be billed to the member.
3. Members must also sign and agree to the supplementary **"liability waiver"**.

## 6. Closed days

1. Facilities may be closed due to **reservations, cleaning, courses, maintenance, workshops**, etc.
2. Facilities may also be closed for **vacations and holidays**.

## 7. Storage

1. Members may store **belongings** in the designated storage box assigned to them individually.
2. Members may temporarily store belongings in the makerspace, provided they obtain **prior approval from the Board** to ensure that the storage does not interfere with the usability of the facilities for other members.
3. Stored belongings without agreement will be **removed after 7 days** at the member's expense.

## 8. Insurance

1. Members are strongly encouraged to have **insurance**.
2. The Forening does **NOT cover injuries or lost/damaged property** of members.
3. Recommended insurances: **Indboforsikring** and **ulykkeforsikring** or equivalents.
4. Members should consult their **insurance company** if in doubt.

## 9. Cleanup

1. Members must **clean up after themselves**.
2. Facilities must be left in at least the same **condition** as when the member started using them.
3. Responsibilities include **sweeping floors, wiping tables, returning utensils, tools, and machines to their original place**.
4. Members are encouraged to **clean up after each other** if something is missed.

## 10. Trash

1. Facility **materials** must be disposed of in the appropriate **container**.
2. Members bringing their own materials are **responsible for disposal** at their own expense.

## 11. Use of machines and tools

1. Members must not operate machines/tools they are **unfamiliar** or **uncomfortable** with.
2. Some machines require a **license** to operate independently or under supervision.
3. Facilities are **common areas**; members are encouraged to give each other space and allow priority if someone is in a hurry.

## 12. Safety

1. Members must **sign the liability waiver** and follow its terms.
2. Members must follow **ALL safety rules and regulations**.
3. **Personal protective equipment** must be used at all times.
4. Members are responsible for preventing **bodily harm or injury** to others.

## 13. Guests

1. Members may bring guests as **company, not workforce**.
2. Members are **responsible and liable** for their guests.
3. Terms of the Forening **apply to guests** as well.

## 14. Dispute Resolution

1. In the scenario of a dispute between members, a formal **complaint** shall be made to the Board via official communication.
   1. The Board shall organise a **conflict hearing** within 14 working days to find a resolution.
   2. The accused member may provide a **written response** to the complaint prior to the hearing.
   3. Should the members involved come to a **resolution** before the hearing, the case shall be rescinded.
2. The Forening uses a **3-strike warning system** to resolve conflicts.
   1. Strike one = **oral and written warning**.
   2. Strike two = **short-term ban**, not exceeding three (3) weeks.
   3. Strike three = **revocation of membership**.
3. In severe cases, the Forening reserves the right to issue an **immediate membership revocation**.
   1. If banned, the member must **remove all belongings** from the facility as soon as possible.
4. One **strike** shall be rescinded from a member after an 18-month period with no further infractions.

## 15. License

1. Certain **machines/tools** require a **license** to operate.
2. The Association hosts opportunities to acquire **licenses**.
3. There are two levels of **licensing**:
   1. **Level 1**: use under guidance of a Level 2 licensed member.
   2. **Level 2**: independent use and ability to supervise Level 1 licensed members.

## 16. Privacy policy

1. The Association's **privacy policy** is in the "Cookie and privacy policy" annex.
