The following terms are used throughout the Articles of Association and other governing documents of O'Town Makerspace.

1. **Association** – refers to O'Town Makerspace.
2. **Board** – refers to the Board of Directors of the Association.
3. **Member** – any individual who has signed the Terms of Membership and is accepted into the Association.
4. **Facilities** – all physical spaces, tools, and equipment managed by the Association.
5. **Annexes** – additional documents referenced in the Articles such as the Terms of Membership, Makerspace Layout, Liability Waiver, etc.
